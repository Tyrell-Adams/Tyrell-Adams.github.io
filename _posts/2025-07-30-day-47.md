what_i_learned: |
  Today I did more research on how to fix the problem of our neural network being too large for ESP32, which is the hardware device we attempted to test on. I learned of another way to combat that problem which was to use an external inference which offloads the heavy task onto another device. From what I researched, this can bypass a great amount of hardware limitations that comes with using an ESP32. The cons that come with this method is that it needs a stable connection, it has high latency, and the power usage would increase because it would be connected to wifi.
