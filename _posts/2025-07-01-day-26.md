what_i_learned: |
  Today I went over my meta-adv model and I decided to see if my model could handle a high amount of epochs. Then that made me think a little deeper into the importance of epochs and how they can improve or hurt a model. I learned to compare epochs to studying, for example, if I go over a paper or my notes once, that's one epoch, and the more times I go over that paper, the more I understand it. During a single epoch, the data set is split into chunks, then it makes predictions, compares predictions, calculates the loss, calculates the gradient, and finally, it updates the weights of the model. I also learned that the weights are a part of the core values that aid the model in making the predictions.
